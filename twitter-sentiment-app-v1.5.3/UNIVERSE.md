# Stock universe

US-listed common shares/ADRs validated against Nasdaq Trader symbol directories on 2026-09-16. Classification is an editorial research taxonomy, not an audited measure of AI revenue. Power, industrial and application themes are intentionally broader than pure-play AI.

357 initial names across eight categories; capacity 1,000. No artificial padding. Market-cap buckets are not claimed without a current capitalization feed. Owner can import reviewed names and disable any ticker in Administration → Stock universe.
